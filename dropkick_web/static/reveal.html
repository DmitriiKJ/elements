Reveal

Open buried commitments in one transaction: a payload of anchors, witness records and claims, signed with SHRINCS. The sandbox assembles it; you only choose the parameters.

DropKick is not active yet
Before Q-day the node does not apply the DropKick rules at all. A reveal is accepted as an ordinary spend even if the commitment is not buried 1440 blocks deep or the payload is invalid. Declare Q-day on the Chain page to see the rules enforced.
