Chain and Q-day

A local regtest node. Mine blocks to bury a commitment, and declare Q-day to make the node reject ordinary spends of hash-protected outputs.

Mining

A reveal is accepted once the commitment's block is at least 1440 blocks below the tip.

Q-day

Q-day restarts the node with DropKick starting at the next period boundary (16 blocks) and mines until the deployment is active. The only way back is a chain reset, which wipes the node and all sandbox data.
